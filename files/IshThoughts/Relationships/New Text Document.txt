IshThoughtsOn - Relationships Intro

People are complex. Maybe too complex to even talk about them in a generalized context. But thats one thing that can be absolutey agreed on- no matter who it is, they're complex. While the complexity stems from an infinite amount of factors, in this section I will focus on how people interact with each other. There are many levels of interation we have with different people, and for every person what each level entails is completely different. We all make assumptions about other people, unconsciously or not. While these assumptions are harmless in itself, they also typically sway our actions in ways- thats where they start becoming an issue.
