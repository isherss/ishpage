A wonderfully crafted album with all of the songs that have defined tsumiki up to the point of release. All of these songs are extremely similar to each other in instrumentation and structure, but all have something unique to give in many ways. There is obviously supposed to be an underlying motif behind all of the songs due to their 'black and white' mvs and discrete numbering/naming extras to each song. 

This album is a must-hear to all vocaloid fans who appreciate the unique sounds the voicebanks bring to songs, and love fast paced songs. 

Listed here will be my entirely subjective ranking of all of the songs in the album including my rationale/tl;dr of the song's analysis from my other documents.

2.レゾンデイトル・カレイドスコウプ
3.リコレクションエンドロウル

#9
スイサイ／アンブレラ／ロクガツ／ドライフラワ

I would like to reiterate that this is absolutely subjective.

I hate to put this at 9th, because it has so many unique aspects to it, such as the many sound effects interjected into the verses, an extremely long instrumental that is separated into two parts, and ending with vocals.
But, the song as a whole sounds unrefined and unfinished when it comes to the rest of the songs on this album. Its almost as if Tsumiki is trying to push how far they can get with their dissonant/random style. The motif that is established in the beginning does not fit the chorus, and instead creates this somewhat jarring transition from the chorus to the post chorus motif section that appears in every other song in this album. 
As for the usually loved instrumental of every other song in this album, this one lacks any flavor. its just chords and nothing of substance. It feels like the filler part of a song- which you never want in a song since they are so short. I want to be able to experience something new with every part of the song. This instrumental made me feel like I was waiting in a loading screen. 
The sound effects that were used in the first verse stayed in the first verse (thank god), they were a pretty jarring noise compared to the calmness of the drums and guitar to me. 
Not only this, but i feel as if the timing between the vocals and instrumentals is just off at some sections of the song. eg. the transition to the second half of the first verse at :42. The tempo and beat the vocals held just did not match the beat of the drums or guitar in the verse. 
I loved the chorus though, and the build up to it was fantastic. It brought the energy and headbopping as always. I really felt the emotion in the vocals for it, and thats something you don't comment on considering vocaloids very often. The ending was also better than usual, without a complete repeat of the beginning in a different voicing- along with the vocals. Everything else was up in the air for me. 
In the end, I feel like this song was the weakest one in the album by FAR. Every other song supercedes this easily.
